
# VaultIQ

VaultIQ is a personal knowledge vault where you can save important YouTube video links and Twitter posts in one organized place. It helps you quickly store, access, and manage the content you find valuable. With a built-in sharing feature, you can easily share your saved videos or tweets with anyone, making VaultIQ a simple and efficient tool for keeping your important content accessible and shareable.

## Features

- Save Important Content: Store YouTube video links and Twitter tweets effortlessly.
- Organized Vault: Keep all saved items neatly categorized and easy to access.
- Dynamic Dashboard: Real-time tracking of sales, orders, and inventory.
- One-Click Sharing: Share any saved video or tweet link with others anytime.
- Clean & Simple UI: Minimal and user-friendly design for smooth navigation.

## Tech Stack

**Client:** React

**Server:** Node, Express

**Database:** MongoDb
